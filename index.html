How SafeSchool works

A simple system designed to identify problems early and protect learning.

📝

1. Report

Students can describe a problem without having to speak in front of classmates.

🔎

2. Review

A designated teacher or counselor reviews reports and follows the school's safeguarding procedures.

🤝

3. Respond

The school can provide support, investigate appropriately, and help students stay focused on learning.

For teachers and counselors

SafeSchool is a communication tool—not a replacement for school safeguarding procedures.

  • Review reports promptly and only with authorized staff.
  • Protect student privacy and avoid sharing identifying information unnecessarily.
  • Follow the school's established anti-bullying and safeguarding protocols.
  • Use weekly check-ins to notice patterns, not to label or publicly identify students.
  • Connect students with appropriate academic and emotional support when needed.
